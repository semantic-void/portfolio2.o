Custom email maintenance system Interactive

Intellect Design Arena, CSOP platform, client bank. Java, Spring Boot, queues, SFTP.

When something changes at a bank, the right person must get the right email. I built the part that works out who that is, across more than 12 situations, and tells the sending system what to send.

  1. Email arrivesAs an XML file and an HTML file.
  2. SplitHTML is shown in the UI. XML goes to a queue.
  3. TransferServer-to-server SFTP sends an encoded string of maintenance values to CS.
  4. RouteChecks if the alert is for a bank, anchor, RM or counterparty: 12+ cases.
  5. TriggerCS is told which custom email to send, with all the data it needs.
Try it: who is the alert for?

Problem

Maintenance emails had to reach the right people with the right template, and the data had to move between two internal systems reliably.

What I built

The intake, the queue and SFTP hand-off to CS, and the routing logic that picks the custom email.
